How to Reset Your Business Portal Password

Reset access securely without sharing a password with Aligned Print & Scan or exposing a recovery link.

Use the official recovery page

  1. Open the Aligned Print & Scan Business Portal sign-in page.
  2. Choose the password-recovery option and enter your invited email address.
  3. If the address is eligible, use the time-limited link in the recovery email.
  4. Create a strong, unique password and return to the portal.
  5. Close other shared-device sessions if the device is not yours.

A privacy-safe response

The recovery form does not confirm whether an email address exists in the system. This helps prevent account discovery. Check spam or filtered folders, allow a few minutes, and submit a new request only when needed.

Aligned Print & Scan never needs your password

Aligned Print & Scan staff cannot see your password and should never ask you to send it. Do not forward a recovery link, one-time code, or session details.

Important: If you no longer control the invited email address, contact Aligned Print & Scan support for an identity and organization-access review. Do not create a second account to bypass access controls.

Frequently asked questions

Why did the form not confirm my account?

The neutral message protects account privacy.

Can Aligned Print & Scan set a password for me?

No. You establish your own credentials through the secure recovery flow.

What if the link expired?

Return to the official recovery page and request a new link.
