Mobile Notary vs. Remote Online Notary

Compare travel, technology, identity steps, document handling, and practical fit before requesting a notary service.

The central difference

A mobile notary travels to an agreed physical location. A remote online notary and signer meet by live audio-video through an approved technology platform while the Texas online notary is physically in Texas.

When mobile may fit

  • The document or receiving party requires a physical appearance or wet-ink original.
  • A signer cannot complete online identity proofing.
  • Technology, internet access, accessibility, or document format makes an online session impractical.
  • Several physical documents or signers need coordinated handling.

When RON may fit

  • The receiving party accepts remote online notarization.
  • The signer has compatible technology and acceptable identification.
  • Travel or scheduling makes a live online appointment more convenient.
  • The document can be completed and delivered electronically through the approved workflow.
Important: Aligned Print & Scan can explain service logistics and fees. Aligned Print & Scan cannot determine which form of notarization a legal transaction requires; confirm that with the receiving party or qualified legal counsel.

Frequently asked questions

Is one option more valid?

Validity depends on the governing law, required act, document, execution, and receiving party—not convenience alone.

Does a mobile notary charge travel?

Mobile service pricing may include travel based on the service location and quoted route.

Can Aligned Print & Scan switch my request?

Ask as early as possible. Eligibility, availability, pricing, and document requirements must be reviewed.
