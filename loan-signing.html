APS Role

A signing-agent workflow, not legal or financial advice.

APS coordinates assignment instructions, package handling, printing, signer or borrower copies, appointment details, required scanbacks, and authorized return steps. APS does not act as lender, title or escrow counsel and does not explain the legal or financial effect of documents.

Signing methods

In person / Mobile: the assignment remains a Loan Signing and uses the designated signing location.

Remote Online Notarization: may be requested where eligible. Remote execution remains subject to document, lender/title, recipient, jurisdiction, signer-identity, and platform approval. Selection does not guarantee eligibility.

Request process

Structured details, reviewed before confirmation.

1. Submit the assignment

Provide the ordering party, file number, signing type and method, property and signing-location details, signers, timing, package state, and special instructions.

2. APS reviews scope

Final scope and pricing depend on the assignment, package, travel, printing, scanback, and authorized return requirements.

3. Coordinate fulfillment

APS confirms the appointment and handles the approved package, copy, scanback, and carrier or direct-return concepts applicable to the assignment.

Cancellation, no-sign, resign, waiting, and other charges are governed by current APS policy and assignment authorization. No live RON transaction is created during intake.
