Our Role

A signing-agent workflow, not legal or financial advice.

We coordinate assignment instructions, package handling, printing, signer or borrower copies, appointment details, required scanbacks, and authorized return steps. Aligned Print & Scan does not act as lender, title or escrow counsel and does not explain the legal or financial effect of documents.

Remote Online Notarization: may be requested where eligible. Remote execution remains subject to document, lender/title, recipient, jurisdiction, signer-identity, and platform approval. Selection does not guarantee eligibility.
