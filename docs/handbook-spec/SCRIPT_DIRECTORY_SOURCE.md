## Required handbook treatment

- Put full cards in Part X and task-focused duplicates/condensations in Part XIII only through cross-reference or controlled reuse.
- Place legal/notarial scripts beside a prominent no-legal-advice boundary and current authoritative-source review date.
- Do not shorten stop conditions in quick references.
- Distinguish a customer communication template (rendered, logged, potentially state-associated) from an operator script/card (read-only guidance).
- Automated publication must import/parse the maintained catalog and assert 48 unique keys and the eight exact categories.

## Source verification required during entry drafting

- The maintained catalog supplies each item’s use situation, stop conditions, and related text; derive WHEN NOT TO USE from explicit boundaries without expanding authority.
- Verify related template links individually. Many scripts appropriately have no customer template.
- Revalidate notarial wording and legal citations against current authoritative sources before publication; do not change maintained production behavior through handbook prose.
