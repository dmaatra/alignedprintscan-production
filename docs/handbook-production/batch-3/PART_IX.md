# PART IX — LOAN SIGNING

## Chapter 29 — Loan Signing Foundations & Workflow

### 29.1 Loan Signing Versus Mobile Notary

A Mobile Notary request centers on one or more notarial acts at a location. A Loan Signing assignment centers on a controlled closing package, ordering-party instructions, presentation/signature requirements, possible notarizations, printing, scanbacks, approval, and physical return. The signing agent remains a notary only for actual notarial acts.

### 29.2 Ordering Party Versus Signer

The ordering party controls assignment scope and operational instructions. The signer controls their own decisions and signatures. Neither relationship authorizes APS to give legal, lending, title, escrow, tax, or financial advice.

### 29.3 Role and Signing-Agent Boundaries

Present documents neutrally, identify printed information, obtain signatures/initials/dates as instructed, perform authorized notarial acts, preserve package integrity, and report exceptions. Refer interpretation, discrepancies, term questions, and decisions to the designated contact.

### 29.4 Signing Types

APS recognizes Buyer/Purchase, Seller, Refinance, HELOC, Loan Modification, Reverse Mortgage, Commercial/Complex, and Other/Custom. The actual order and package determine scope.

### 29.5 Signing Methods

In-person/mobile is the default physical package workflow. RON is used only when the assignment, document, receiving party, platform, law, and APS readiness permit it. “Either/TBD” is a blocker until the authorized method is confirmed.

### 29.6 Property and Signing Addresses

Keep property address and signing location separate. Verify the actual appointment location, route, access, safety, timezone, and property reference without assuming they are the same.

### 29.7 Company, Order, and Escrow Identifiers

Record the ordering company, company file/order number, escrow/transaction number, and other authorized identifiers exactly. Do not place unnecessary account or taxpayer data in ordinary notes.

### 29.8 Structured Signers

Maintain each signer’s full legal name, role/capacity, contact path, confirmation requirement, and relationship to documents. Do not collapse multiple signers into a note.

### 29.9 Eight Broad APS Stages

Assignment review → package readiness → printing/QC → appointment readiness → signing → scanbacks/approval → return → completion. The ten-gate framework in Chapter 35 adds financial and exception resolution controls.

### 29.10 Sensitive Information

Protect loan numbers, taxpayer data, bank details, IDs, nonpublic personal information, and package contents. Use approved storage/communication, not ordinary email or screenshots.

### 29.11 Loan Signing Workflow Map

Order received → assignment/instructions reviewed → quote/authorization → package/version received → print/QC → appointment/signer confirmation → table signing/notarization → table check → scanback/approval when required → physical return/tracking when required → exception/financial resolution → closeout.

## Chapter 30 — Loan Package Types

### 30.1 Buyer / Purchase

Purpose: transfer/acquire property while funding a new loan and completing settlement. Expect borrower loan documents, title/settlement documents, possible deed-side documents, disclosures, affidavits, and property/product riders.

| Document | What it is / why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / referral |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Closing Disclosure | final disclosed loan/cost summary | reviews and signs/acknowledges | usually no | signatures only; refer figures |
| Note | promise to repay | signs | usually no | names, dates, package version |
| Security Instrument | secures debt with property | owners/borrowers sign | commonly; inspect certificate | capacities, riders, certificate |
| Title/Settlement forms | closing/disbursement/title requirements | signs assigned forms | varies | preserve order; refer title questions |
| Occupancy/Name affidavits | transaction representations | reviews and signs | often varies | no coaching; actual certificate controls |

### 30.2 Seller

Seller packages center on conveyance, title/settlement, payoff, proceeds, identity, and seller representations. They may be shorter than borrower packages but can contain high-consequence deeds and affidavits.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deed | conveyance | grantor signs | commonly | exact capacity/certificate; no deed advice |
| Seller affidavits | title/transaction facts | reads, supplies facts, signs | frequently varies | no factual coaching |
| Settlement statement | proceeds/cost accounting | reviews/signs | usually no | refer financial discrepancies |
| Payoff authorization | permits payoff information/action | signs | varies | protect account information |
| Closing instructions | controls execution/return | follows assigned acknowledgments | varies | orderer controls |

### 30.3 Refinance

Refinance replaces or changes financing on existing property. Packages commonly include Closing Disclosure, note, security instrument, rescission notice when applicable, escrow/payment information, affidavits, riders, and title documents.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Closing Disclosure | final terms/costs | reviews/signs | usually no | do not interpret figures |
| Note | repayment obligation | signs | usually no | version/signature |
| Security Instrument | collateral | signs | commonly | actual certificate |
| Right to Cancel | covered rescission notice | dates/acknowledges receipt | no | do not calculate disputed deadline |
| Affidavits/Compliance | representations/corrections | signs | varies | refer legal questions |

### 30.4 HELOC

HELOC packages concern a revolving line secured by property. Actual Texas home-equity and lender instructions are critical; never generalize product or legal requirements.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HELOC Agreement/Note | line terms/repayment | signs/initials | usually no | no rate/draw advice |
| HELOC Security Instrument | secures line | owners sign | commonly | actual certificate/capacity |
| Disclosures | product/cost information | acknowledges | usually no | refer lender questions |
| Right to Cancel when applicable | rescission notice | acknowledges/dates | no | printed instructions control |

### 30.5 Loan Modification

Modification packages document lender/servicer-approved changes to an existing obligation. Do not imply that signing guarantees a particular servicing outcome.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Modification Agreement | states approved changes | signs | may be | correct loan/borrower/version |
| Affidavits/Certifications | supports eligibility/facts | reads/signs | varies | no coaching |
| Payment Instructions | operational next step | reviews/retains | no | do not accept payment unless authorized |
| Security-related instrument | records/continues collateral change | signs | often varies | actual certificate controls |

### 30.6 Reverse Mortgage

Reverse Mortgage packages may contain multiple notes/security instruments, a loan agreement, payment-plan disclosures, certifications, riders, and non-borrowing-spouse documents. Provide time and neutral organization; refer counseling/product questions.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note(s) | evidence debt structure | borrowers sign | usually no | paired documents/version |
| Mortgage(s)/Security Instruments | secure obligations | required parties sign | commonly | paired certificates/riders |
| Loan Agreement/Disclosures | program terms/responsibilities | reviews/signs | varies | no product advice |
| NBS documents | defined spouse capacity | named spouse signs | varies | never determine eligibility/rights |

### 30.7 Commercial / Custom

Commercial/custom packages vary by entity, authority, counsel, property, and negotiated terms. Require explicit instructions and capacity evidence; source-note unfamiliar instruments.

| Document | Why it appears | Signer usually does | Commonly notarized? | Operator focus / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note/Security documents | debt/collateral | authorized representative signs | varies | entity/capacity and certificate |
| Resolutions/Authority | proves signing authority | signs/certifies if directed | varies | do not decide sufficiency |
| Guarantees | supports obligation | guarantor signs | varies | no legal explanation |
| Custom closing documents | negotiated requirements | signs as assigned | varies | ordering party/counsel controls |

## Chapter 32 — Loan Signing Intake & Assignment Review

### 32.1–32.4 Order, Parties, Identifiers, and Addresses

Verify orderer/customer, all structured signers/capacities, company/order/escrow identifiers, property address, signing address, date/time/timezone, access, and contact path.

### 32.5–32.8 Scope and Instructions

Confirm signing type/method, package status, print requirements/copies, signer confirmation, scanbacks, approval-before-return, physical return, shipping label, stipulations, and source of every instruction.

### 32.9 Assignment Review Procedure

1. Open the exact request and Loan Signing assignment.
2. Compare order, signers, identifiers, addresses, and appointment.
3. Read all authoritative instructions; enter each requirement with source and applicability.
4. Identify missing/conflicting facts and stop advancement.
5. Record review completion only after scope is coherent.

## Chapter 33 — Preparing the Loan Signing Quote

### 33.1–33.5 Quote Inputs

Verify package type/complexity, page count/version, print sets and borrower copies, appointment/travel, scanbacks, approval hold, return method, after-hours/rush, and authorized custom items. Do not price from an unreceived package when print volume materially controls price unless the uncertainty is explicit and authorized.

### 33.6–33.9 Review, Approval, Payment, Revision

Review the customer/orderer-facing scope, send the current version, record authorized approval, and satisfy prepaid or authorized Business terms. Revise for package, appointment, printing, scanback, return, wait/resign, or other scope change without rewriting paid history.

|---|---|---|---|

| Information/package needed | request exact missing item | orderer-directed message | blocker resolved |

| Print/QC | print active version and inspect | readiness/issue message | print gate passes |
| Appointment | confirm signer/time/location | confirmation/reminder | signer/appointment ready |

| Return | package/label/drop-off/tracking | tracking/return notice | return evidence complete |
| Exception/financial | record cause and decision | approved exception message | operational/financial resolution |
| Completion | verify all gates | closeout communication | ten gates pass |

## Chapter 35 — Loan Signing Fulfillment Gates

### 35.1 Ten-Gate Framework

1. Assignment Review.
2. Pricing / Financial Readiness.
3. Package Readiness.
4. Printing / QC.
5. Appointment Readiness.
6. Signing Completion.
7. Scanback / Approval.
8. Physical Return.
9. Exception / Financial Resolution.
10. Final Completion.

### 35.2 Gate Rule

Each gate records purpose, required conditions, operator location, APS display, blocker, communication, authorized override if any, prohibited bypass, and next stage. “Not applicable” must be supported by assignment facts—not selected to make completion easier.

## Chapter 36 — Package Preparation & Printing

### 36.1 Package Version Decision Tree

New file received? Compare source/order/timestamp/instructions. Explicit replacement? Preserve prior version, mark new active version, recalculate page/print facts, and repeat QC. Ambiguous? Hold and contact orderer. Never combine pages across versions without written authority.

### 36.2 Authoritative Page Count and Print Volume

Source pages × signing sets/borrower/additional copies determines expected printed pages; sidedness informs expected sheets. Use verified source count and record the package version.

### 36.3 Print and QC Procedure

1. Confirm active version and instructions.
2. Verify page size, mixed letter/legal needs, scale, sidedness, color, sets, and borrower copies.
3. Print one controlled set; compare count/order/quality.
4. Print remaining authorized sets.
5. Conduct final count/order/legibility/orientation check.
6. Keep copies separated and protected.

## Chapter 37 — Conducting the Loan Signing

### 37.1–37.3 Before Leaving, What to Bring, Arrival

Verify gates, active package, copies, journal/seal, IDs/tools, contact/escalation path, route, return materials, phone power, and safety. At arrival confirm signer/location before exposing documents.

### 37.4–37.9 Identity Through Common Document Presentation

Establish satisfactory identity for notarial acts, confirm signer willingness and capacity without legal conclusions, present package in required order, obtain exact signatures/initials/dates, complete only actual certificates, and preserve document order.

### 37.10–37.13 Signer Questions and Ordering-Party Contact

You may identify a document, point to printed information, repeat authorized instructions, and explain notarial procedure. Refer meaning, correctness, loan terms, settlement figures, title, tax, legal effect, or whether to sign. Pause and contact the designated orderer; record the outcome.

### 37.14–37.16 Stipulations, Table Check, Departure

Complete authorized stipulations exactly. Before departure, inspect every required signature/initial/date/certificate, page order, loose items, copies, journal, scanback set, and return package. Do not leave with a correctable omission unnoticed.

## Chapter 38 — Scanbacks, Approval & Return

### 38.1–38.4 Requirements, Selection, Upload, Rescan

Identify whether scanbacks are required and which pages/set. Scan in order, legibly, at the required resolution; open/QC the final file. If rejected or incomplete, rescan from the signed originals and preserve the correction trail.

### 38.5–38.7 Approval Before Return

When approval-before-return applies, hold the physical package securely. Follow up through the approved channel and wait. Never return while the gate is unresolved.

### 38.8–38.10 Physical Return and Completion Readiness

Use the authorized label/carrier/drop-off method. Verify package, address/label, cutoff, receipt, tracking, and proof. Completion requires return evidence where applicable.

### 38.11 Scanback → Approval → Return Decision Tree

Scanbacks required? If NO, follow return instruction. If YES, scan/QC/upload. Approval required? If YES, hold securely until explicit approval. Rescan requested? Correct and re-upload. Once approved, return using the authorized label, retain receipt/tracking, and record evidence.

### 38.12 Non-Negotiable Hold

**STOP — Never return while an applicable approval-before-return gate is unresolved.**

## Chapter 39 — Loan Signing Exceptions & Financial Resolution

### 39.1–39.5 Cancellation, No-Sign, Partial, Resign, Cause

Record what occurred, when, who directed the stop, documents affected, package custody, and communication. Distinguish APS-caused correction from signer/orderer/document/other causes without making blame statements.

### 39.6 Excessive Wait

The maintained suggestion includes the first 30 minutes, then $25 per started additional 30-minute increment. APS-caused delay is excluded by default. An administrator authorizes any charge, waiver, or custom result; the operator does not collect automatically onsite.

### 39.7–39.11 Stops, Review, Supplemental Invoice, Refund, Resolution

Identity, package, signer, instruction, safety, or legal-boundary stops take priority. Additional amounts use the authorized supplemental-invoice workflow; refunds remain tied to original payment and provider evidence.

### 39.12 Loan Signing Exception Decision Tree

Can the signing safely and lawfully continue under current instructions? If YES, correct/continue and record. If NO, stop and contact orderer. Is same-visit correction authorized? If NO, secure package and follow no-sign/partial/resign direction. Determine cause and financial review separately. Resume or close only after both operational and financial resolution.

### 39.13 Resign Rule

APS-caused resign suggestion is zero. Other causes require administrator review. Link every additional visit and package version to the original assignment.

## Chapter 40 — Loan Signing Closeout & Completion

### 40.1–40.5 Completion Evidence

Operational work, scanback/approval, return/tracking, exceptions, and financial resolution each require evidence when applicable.

### 40.6–40.9 Communication, Timeline, Gate, History

Send only the approved closeout communication for the correct audience. Review Timeline for assignment, package, print, appointment, signing, scans, approval, return, exceptions, money, and final completion. Preserve all versions and history.

### 40.10 Closeout Checklist

- Active package and all instructions reconciled.
- Signatures/initials/dates/certificates and table check complete.
- Scanbacks/QC/approval complete when applicable.
- Package returned with receipt/tracking when applicable.
- Exceptions and supplemental/refund decisions resolved.
- Required payment or authorized terms correctly recorded.
- Ordering-party/signer communication and Timeline verified.
- Final completion gate passed without override abuse.
