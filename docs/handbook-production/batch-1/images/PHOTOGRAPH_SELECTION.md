# Batch 1 Photograph Selection

Batch 1 identifies five candidate subjects: professional Black APS operator/workstation; Black customer preparing synthetic documents; older Black customer reviewing synthetic paperwork; Black/P.O.C. customer using a safe portal fixture; and a professional document-review environment.

No photograph is included in the preview until rights, model/property releases, source metadata, caption purpose, alt text, and privacy review are complete. The diagram/screenshot-led preview therefore records photography as **SOURCE/RIGHTS VERIFICATION REQUIRED**, not as a failed representation standard. Final selection must feature Black people prominently, include broader P.O.C. representation, vary age/skin tone/hair/gender/role/context, and avoid repetitive same-model imagery.
