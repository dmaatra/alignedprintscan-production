Device access & preparation

Prepare your device, document, and identification.

Use a supported phone, tablet, or computer with a working camera, microphone, modern browser, stable connection, and a private, well-lit space.

  • Your signing-ready document and receiving-party instructions
  • An acceptable, unexpired government-issued photo ID
  • Access to your email and phone

Identity verification

Credential analysis and identity proofing are separate checks.

Approved technology evaluates an identification credential. Identity proofing may also include dynamic knowledge-based authentication (KBA). The provider creates and evaluates those questions; the notary cannot see answers, supply answers, or override a result.

If a check is unsuccessful

An unsuccessful result is not a character judgment. Follow the platform's neutral instructions. A permitted retry, correction, or in-person option may be available; no result is promised.

Location & eligibility

The Texas online notary must be in Texas.

The signer may be elsewhere, but the notary must be physically located in Texas during the act. The document, receiving party, technology, signer circumstances, and applicable law must also allow remote notarization.

Before booking: confirm that the receiving party accepts remote online notarization and identifies the notarial act it requires.

Frequently asked questions

Remote Online Notary FAQs

Can I complete the session from my phone?

Often, yes, when the platform supports it and the camera, microphone, browser, and connection work reliably.

What ID should I have?

Have an acceptable, unexpired government-issued photo ID ready for the secure credential-analysis flow.

Should I sign before the appointment?

Not automatically. A jurat requires signing in the notary's presence; an acknowledgment follows a different procedure. Follow the receiving party's instructions or wait for procedural direction.

Is the session recorded?

Texas online notarization requires an audio-video record and electronic records under applicable requirements. The secure platform manages those records.

Can acceptance be guaranteed?

No. Acceptance is controlled by the receiving party and applicable law. Confirm acceptance before the appointment.
