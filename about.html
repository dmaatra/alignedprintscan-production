Professional Standards & Affiliations

Role-specific standards for professional notary service.

Aligned Print & Scan (APS) maintains role-specific professional standards for our notary professionals. APS notaries maintain active membership with the National Notary Association (NNA), supporting access to professional resources, education and industry developments.

All APS Loan Signing Agents hold the NNA Certified Notary Signing Agent credential and satisfy APS qualification requirements for performing loan signing assignments.
